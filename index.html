Privacy & limits

  • Three free builds and 30 edits a day per person while Sogilex Build is in beta. Builds or edits that fail don't count.
  • Your description, and any change you ask for, is sent to Anthropic to generate or edit the site. We don't keep the description. We keep each finished site and each edited version so you can reopen and publish it, with a one-line note of what each edit changed, and we record when builds happen and how long they take, to manage costs.
  • Publishing (beta) puts your site at your-name.sogilex.net. Messages sent through its contact form are stored for you and shown in Build, and emailed to the address you choose (any address other than your sign-in email has to be confirmed from its inbox first). Published sites carry a small "Made with Sogilex" footer with a link to report abuse.
  • Your photos are resized in your browser (which also removes hidden details such as where a photo was taken) and stored privately. Small previews are sent to Anthropic so Claude can place them. They become public only on a site you publish.
  • Facts stay yours to fill in. Build never invents prices, phone numbers, addresses or reviews. It leaves placeholders like [PHONE NUMBER] for you to replace.
  • No logins or look-alikes. Build won't make pages that imitate real companies or collect passwords or card details.
  • The site is yours. Use, edit and publish what you download however you like.
  • The full details are in the privacy policy and terms of use.
