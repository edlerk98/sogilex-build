How it works

  1. 01

    Describe

    A sentence or two about what the site is for, plus a style and a colour.

  2. 02

    Watch it build

    Claude, Anthropic's AI, designs the page and writes it while you watch the preview fill in.

  3. 03

    Download

    One self-contained HTML file. Host it anywhere: GitHub Pages, Netlify, your own server.

Privacy & limits

  • Three free builds a day per person while Sogilex Build is in beta.
  • Your description is sent to Anthropic to generate the site. We don't keep the description. We keep each finished site so you can reopen and publish it, and we record when builds happen and how long they take, to manage costs.
  • Publishing (beta) puts your site at your-name.sogilex.net. Messages sent through its contact form are stored for you and shown in Build. Published sites carry a small "Made with Sogilex" footer with a link to report abuse.
  • Facts stay yours to fill in. Build never invents prices, phone numbers, addresses or reviews. It leaves placeholders like [PHONE NUMBER] for you to replace.
  • No logins or look-alikes. Build won't make pages that imitate real companies or collect passwords or card details.
  • The site is yours. Use, edit and publish what you download however you like.
